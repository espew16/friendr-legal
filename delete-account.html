Delete your Friendr account

You can delete your Friendr account and data at any time.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Friendr and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete forever.

Your account is deleted right away.

Can't get into the app?

Email friendrsupport@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject "Delete my Friendr account." We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, birthday, bio, prompts, interests, and preferences.
  • Your photos.
  • Your approximate location.
  • Your matches and chat messages.
  • Your posts, likes, and comments.
  • Your login (email and password).

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Residual copies in backups may remain for a limited time before being overwritten. For more detail, see our Privacy Policy.

Friendr

Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Friendr helps adults find new platonic friends. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have. Friendr is operated by Andrew Finklang ("we", "us"). If you have questions, email friendrsupport@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Account information. Your email address and a password. Your password is stored in a hashed form by our authentication provider; we cannot read it.

Profile information you add. Your first name, birthday, up to four photos, a short bio, your answers to profile prompts, your interests, what kind of friendships you are looking for, and your preferred search distance.

Approximate location. If you allow it, Friendr reads your device location when you open the Discover screen. Before saving, the app rounds it to two decimal places (roughly one kilometer), so we never store your exact position. You can turn location off in your phone settings; Friendr will still work, but you will not be able to see people near you.

Activity in the app. People you like or pass on, your matches, messages you send, posts you share, likes and comments on posts, people you block, and reports you submit.

Reports. If you report someone, we store the reason, any note you add, and up to the last 20 messages in your chat with that person, so we can investigate.

Technical information. Our service providers may log basic technical data such as IP address, device type, and timestamps to keep the service secure and working.

We do not currently use advertising networks or third-party analytics tools in the app. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account and operate Friendr, including showing you to other people and showing you people near you.
  • To calculate your age from your birthday and to confirm you are 18 or older.
  • To deliver matches, messages, posts, and comments.
  • To keep Friendr safe: reviewing reports, enforcing our Terms, preventing spam and abuse, and responding to legal requests.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

3. What other people can see

  • Other signed-in users can see your first name, age (never your birthday), photos, bio, prompts, interests, what you are looking for, and your approximate distance in miles. They never see your email address or your coordinates.
  • People you have matched with can message you and can see your posts, likes, and comments in the feed. Their matches can also see your comments on their posts.
  • Photos are stored privately and shown through temporary links available only to signed-in users.
  • Messages are visible to you and the person you are chatting with. They are not end-to-end encrypted. Authorized personnel may review messages when needed to handle a report, protect someone's safety, or comply with the law.

4. Who we share information with

  • Service providers. We use Supabase to host our database, handle sign-in, and store photos. These providers process data only to provide their services to us.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law, to respond to valid legal process, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.
  • Business changes. If Friendr is ever sold or merged, user information may transfer to the new owner, who must honor this policy.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, we permanently delete your profile, photos, matches, messages, posts, likes, and comments. Residual copies in backups may remain for a limited time before being overwritten.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile information at any time from the Profile tab.
  • Delete your account from Profile, then Delete account. You can also request deletion by email; see how to delete your account.
  • Location: allow or deny it in your phone's settings.
  • Block or report any user from Discover or a chat.

Depending on where you live (for example California or other US states, the UK, or the European Economic Area), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain uses. To exercise any of these rights, email us at friendrsupport@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by law.

7. Age requirement

Friendr is only for people 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete the account and its data. If you believe a minor is using Friendr, please report the profile or email us.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls on our database. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International users

Friendr is operated from the United States, and your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you in the app or by email. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
